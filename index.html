FROM A NOTE TO A CONVERSATION

So, what happens next?

  1. 01

    You share.

    A course issue, a suggestion, something that could work better. Give us enough detail to understand it.

  2. 02

    Your reps listen.

    Your note is emailed privately to the representatives’ inbox. Leave an email if you’d like them to follow up.

  3. 03

    We take it forward.

    The Student–Staff Liaison Committee brings reps and staff together to discuss teaching, learning and the student experience.

A PRIVATE NOTE, NOT A PUBLIC POST

Your note & your privacy.

Your feedback is sent directly to FormSubmit for processing and email delivery to the PPE student representatives. FormSubmit says it retains submissions for 30 days. Copies received in the reps’ inbox are managed separately by the representatives.

This site does not put your note on the board, save it in a GitHub repository, or store it in your browser. It has no analytics or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages serves the site; your browser connects to FormSubmit when you submit.

Your name and email are optional. Without an email, reps cannot reply directly. Avoid including sensitive personal information or details that identify other people. Reps may discuss relevant course issues with staff.

FormSubmit privacy policy ↗ GitHub privacy statement ↗

Feedback stays in this page only while it is open. A delivery error keeps your entries here so you can try again. Retrying after an uncertain result may send a duplicate; the same reference helps reps recognise it.
