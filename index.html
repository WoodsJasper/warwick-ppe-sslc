ABOUT THE SSLC

What happens to your feedback?

  1. 01

    Submit the form

    Describe the course issue or suggestion. Module codes and specific examples help us understand what you mean.

  2. 02

    Your reps review it

    Your feedback is emailed privately to the representatives’ inbox. If you leave an email, reps can contact you for more information or follow up with you.

  3. 03

    Reps discuss course issues with staff

    The Student–Staff Liaison Committee brings reps and staff together to discuss teaching, learning and the student experience.

For personal support, formal complaints or assessment appeals, please use the relevant University service. Warwick Wellbeing Support ↗

PRIVACY

How your feedback is handled

Your feedback is sent directly to FormSubmit for processing and email delivery to the PPE student representatives. FormSubmit says it retains submissions for 30 days. Copies received in the reps’ inbox are managed separately by the representatives.

This site does not publish your feedback, save it in a GitHub repository, or save a draft in your browser. It has no analytics or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages serves the site; your browser connects to FormSubmit when you submit.

Your name and email are optional. Without an email, reps cannot reply directly. Avoid including sensitive personal information or details that identify other people. Reps may discuss relevant course issues with staff.
